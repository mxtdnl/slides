What your firm decides

Three decisions, every quarter

1

Feed

How hard your feed pushes: CHRONO, RANKED, AUTOPLAY or STREAKS. A higher feed builds reach faster.

Starts at FEED 2, RANKED

2

Safeguards

Age checks, time limits, quiet hours and moderation: 0% to 30% of the reference budget. They cost money. They lower public exposure and the risk of a harm story.

Starts at 10%

3

Card

One optional action. The same card cannot repeat in consecutive quarters.

Starts at no card

These are the only three things your firm sets. FEED is how hard your feed pushes: 1 CHRONO is a chronological feed, 2 RANKED a ranked feed, 3 AUTOPLAY adds autoplay and infinite scroll, 4 STREAKS adds streaks, variable rewards and late-night notifications. A higher feed builds reach faster, and costs more. SAFEGUARDS is your spend on age checks, time limits, quiet hours and moderation, set as a percentage of a reference budget. A card is a one-off move, covered in two slides. Each quarter starts from your previous settings, so you only change what you want to change. About 1 minute.

The card

Six cards, at most one a quarter

CLONECopy a rival’s feature

Take reach from a named rival. Needs a target.

PUBLISHRelease internal wellbeing research

Raises public trust. Lowers your harm-story risk this quarter.

LOBBYLobby the Assembly

Exempt from code fines detected this quarter. Lowers public trust.

BLITZInfluencer campaign

Raises your effective reach this quarter only.

SHARERelease safety tooling to the industry

Lowers every platform’s harm-story risk this quarter, yours included.

LAUNCHShip a new engagement feature

Raises your reach. Raises your public exposure and harm-story risk this quarter.

Your estimate includes the card’s cost before you commit. The same card cannot repeat in consecutive quarters.

These are the card effects as your phone shows them. No card is always allowed. CLONE needs a target, chosen in the card picker. SHARE protects every platform, yours included. LAUNCH gains reach for good but raises your exposure and harm-story risk that quarter. LOBBY refers to youth design codes (slide 15). Each card's cost is in the estimate before you commit. About 1 minute.

On the shared screen · The board

The shared screen shows every firm at once

Screenshot from a practice run with made-up firms. Firms marked BOT are run by the computer.

Step through the parts with r. The board is public: every firm sees every other firm's share, profit and valuation, but not their cash, feed or safeguards. On the shared screen · After each quarter

The shared screen names every harm story

Screenshot from a practice run with made-up firms.

This appears when the facilitator resolves a quarter in which any platform had a harm story: leaked internal research, whistleblower testimony or an investigation into harm to teenagers. Step through the parts with r. The harm stories block replaces the trust chart until the next quarter opens: for each story it names the firm, the headline, what it cost, both to public trust and to that firm's revenue that quarter, and what raises the risk. The firm is tagged HARM on the board. The facilitator can list every harm story so far by typing HARM. About 1 minute.
On your phone · After each quarter

Your result arrives on your phone

When the facilitator resolves the quarter, every phone switches to this card at the same moment the shared screen updates. Read the first sentence, then the figures. Notices tell you about anything that happened to your firm alone, such as a harm story or a card that could not be played. The BOOK tab keeps your history quarter by quarter, and compares your valuation with every other firm's. About 1 minute.
On your phone · YDC

Platforms can sign youth design codes

Joining and leaving are public and appear on the wire.

Youth design codes are optional. Any firm can propose one while a quarter is open. The form opens with no term ticked: tick a maximum feed, minimum safeguards, or both, and the line above SIGN shows exactly what your firm is signing. Other firms join from the YDC tab and may leave at any time. Once your firm has signed, the decision ticket warns you if your settings would break the terms; it never stops you committing. The facilitator may also call an industry summit: the timer pauses and every phone opens this tab. Codes can be audited in any quarter, and breaches an audit finds are published and fined. About 1 minute.
Joining

Join in three steps

The shared screen will show a four-letter code, a QR code and the web address. Enter the code on your phone, or scan the QR code, then confirm it with Continue. One person founds the firm and passes the PIN to the rest of the firm; everyone else chooses Join a firm and types the PIN. Do not post the PIN in the main Zoom chat: anyone who has it can join your firm. Device initials are optional; never type a full name. If a phone dies, rejoin with the same PIN. About 1 minute.
