Each quarter your firm decides how fast to deploy, how much to spend on safety, and whether to play a card. The market prices the result.

Welcome. In a moment you will run a fictional AI company, competing with the other firms in this room over a series of quarters. This briefing takes about 15 minutes: how the market works, what your firm decides, and how to use your phone and read the projector. There is one quick question at the start and one check near the end. Keep phones away until the last slide. About 30 seconds.

What your firm decides

Three decisions, every quarter

1

Pace

How fast you deploy: Cautious, Standard, Aggressive or Breakneck. Pace builds capability faster.

Starts at Standard

2

Safety

Spend from 0% to 30% of the reference budget. It costs money. It lowers public exposure and incident risk.

Starts at 10%

3

Card

One optional action. The same card cannot repeat in consecutive quarters.

Starts at no card

These are the only three things your firm sets. Pace is how fast your firm deploys: faster builds capability faster, and costs more. Safety is your safety spend, set as a percentage of a reference budget. A card is a one-off move, covered in two slides. Each quarter starts from your previous settings, so you only change what you want to change. About 1 minute.

The card

Four cards, at most one a quarter

POACHPoach talent

Take capability from a target firm. Needs a target.

PUBLISHPublish safety research

Raises public trust. Lowers your incident risk this quarter.

LOBBYLobby regulators

Exempt from pact fines detected this quarter. Lowers public trust.

BLITZMarketing blitz

Raises your effective capability this quarter only.

A card adds to your costs. The same card cannot repeat in consecutive quarters.

These are the card effects exactly as your phone shows them. Playing no card is always allowed and costs nothing. POACH needs a target: you choose the firm in the card picker. LOBBY refers to pacts, which come up on slide 14. Your phone shows the card's effect under its name, and its cost is included in the estimate before you commit. About 1 minute.

On the projector · The board

The projector shows every firm at once

Screenshot from a practice run with made-up firms. Firms marked BOT are run by the computer.

Step through the parts with r. The board is public: every firm sees every other firm's share, profit and valuation, but not their cash, pace or safety. The tick column shows who has committed this quarter. Firms tagged BOT are run by the computer and may be added to fill the market. The facilitator can switch to other views, such as the full trust history or the pact table, by typing on the projector. About 2 minutes.
On your phone · After each quarter

Your result arrives on your phone

When the facilitator resolves the quarter, every phone switches to this card at the same moment the projector updates. Read the first sentence, then the figures. Notices tell you about anything that happened to your firm alone, such as an incident or a card that could not be played. The BOOK tab keeps your history quarter by quarter, and compares your valuation with every other firm's. About 1 minute.
On your phone · PACTS

Firms can sign pacts with each other

Joining and leaving are public and appear on the wire.

Pacts are optional. Any firm can propose one while a quarter is open, and other firms join from this tab and may leave at any time. The terms are a maximum pace, a minimum safety spend, or both. The facilitator may also call an industry summit: the timer pauses and every phone opens this tab. Pacts can be audited in any quarter, and breaches an audit finds are published and fined. About 1 minute.
Joining

Join in three steps

The projector will show a four-letter code, a QR code and the web address. One person per team founds the firm and reads the PIN to teammates; everyone else chooses Join a firm and types the PIN. Keep the PIN inside your team. Device initials are optional; never type a full name. If a phone dies, rejoin with the same PIN. About 1 minute.
