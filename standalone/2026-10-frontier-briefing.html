Read the chain left to right with the connecting words. Two numbers matter to every firm: the size of the market, which moves with public trust, and your share of it, which moves with your capability. Profit goes into cash; cash and capability make your valuation; the board ranks firms by valuation. A firm that falls behind on capability loses share. Point to the two notes: every cost comes from a decision your firm makes (pace, safety spend and any card) and comes straight out of cash. A firm whose cash falls too far below zero is insolvent: it stays on the board, marked INSOLV, held at Cautious pace with no cards. About 2 minutes.
What your firm decides

Three decisions, every quarter

1

Pace

How fast you deploy: Cautious, Standard, Aggressive or Breakneck. Pace builds capability faster.

Starts at Standard

2

Safety

Spend from 0% to 30% of the reference budget. It costs money. It lowers public exposure and incident risk.

Starts at 10%

3

Card

One optional action. The same card cannot repeat in consecutive quarters.

Starts at no card

These are the only three things your firm sets. Pace is how fast your firm deploys: faster builds capability faster, and costs more. Safety is your safety spend, set as a percentage of a reference budget. A card is a one-off move, covered in two slides. Each quarter starts from your previous settings, so you only change what you want to change. About 1 minute.

The estimate (part 5) updates as you change pace, safety and the card, so you can see the cost before you commit. The card

Six cards, at most one a quarter

POACHPoach talent

Take capability from a target firm. Needs a target.

PUBLISHPublish safety research

Raises public trust. Lowers your incident risk this quarter.

LOBBYLobby regulators

Exempt from pact fines detected this quarter. Lowers public trust.

BLITZMarketing blitz

Raises your effective capability this quarter only.

SHAREShare safety tooling

Lowers every firm’s incident risk this quarter, yours included.

RUSHRush a release

Raises your capability. Raises your public exposure and incident risk this quarter.

Your estimate includes the card’s cost before you commit. The same card cannot repeat in consecutive quarters.

These are the card effects exactly as your phone shows them. Playing no card is always allowed and costs nothing. POACH needs a target: you choose the firm in the card picker. SHARE protects every firm, including yours. RUSH gains capability for good, but raises your public exposure and incident risk for that quarter. LOBBY refers to pacts, which come up on slide 15. Your phone shows the card's effect under its name, and its cost is included in the estimate before you commit. About 1 minute.

On the projector · The board

The projector shows every firm at once

Screenshot from a practice run with made-up firms. Firms marked BOT are run by the computer.

Step through the parts with r. The board is public: every firm sees every other firm's share, profit and valuation, but not their cash, pace or safety. The tick column shows who has committed this quarter. Firms tagged BOT are run by the computer and may be added to fill the market. The facilitator can switch to other views, such as the full trust history or the pact table, by typing on the projector. About 2 minutes.
On the projector · After each quarter

The projector names every incident

Screenshot from a practice run with made-up firms.

This appears when the facilitator resolves a quarter in which any firm had an incident. Step through the parts with r. The incidents block replaces the trust chart until the next quarter opens: for each incident it names the firm, the headline, what it cost, both to public trust and to that firm's revenue that quarter, and what raises the risk. The firm is tagged INCID on the board. The facilitator can list every incident so far by typing INCID. About 1 minute.
On your phone · After each quarter

Your result arrives on your phone

When the facilitator resolves the quarter, every phone switches to this card at the same moment the projector updates. Read the first sentence, then the figures. Notices tell you about anything that happened to your firm alone, such as an incident or a card that could not be played. The BOOK tab keeps your history quarter by quarter, and compares your valuation with every other firm's. About 1 minute.
On your phone · PACTS

Firms can sign pacts with each other

Joining and leaving are public and appear on the wire.

Pacts are optional. Any firm can propose one while a quarter is open. The form opens with no term ticked: tick a maximum pace, a minimum safety spend, or both, and the line above SIGN shows exactly what your firm is signing. Other firms join from this tab and may leave at any time. Once your firm has signed, the decision ticket warns you if your settings would break the terms; it never stops you committing. The facilitator may also call an industry summit: the timer pauses and every phone opens this tab. Pacts can be audited in any quarter, and breaches an audit finds are published and fined. About 1 minute.
Joining

Join in three steps

The projector will show a four-letter code, a QR code and the web address. One person per team founds the firm and reads the PIN to teammates; everyone else chooses Join a firm and types the PIN. Keep the PIN inside your team. Device initials are optional; never type a full name. If a phone dies, rejoin with the same PIN. About 1 minute.
