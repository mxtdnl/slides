Stress that partners are more than clients: in the Social Phenomena Challenge you engage with the issue the charity works on; in the Experiment Challenge the research must be academically grounded and also useful to the partner; in the Propaganda Challenge the think tank may be a source of expertise or a client for messaging; in the Policy Challenge civil service professionals give input and guidance. Coaching is a distinctive part of the degree, so give it its own moment. Capstone options

Three ways to finish

1

Social Action Project

Work inside a community organisation tackling a real social challenge, from food poverty to youth empowerment, as an active contributor.

Reflection, ethical engagement and a civic identity.

2

Multi-Stakeholder Simulation

Represent governments, corporations or civil society in a large-scale simulation, negotiating against a live dashboard of consequences.

Systems thinking and strategic reasoning under uncertainty.

3

Senior Thesis

An independent research project: a dissertation of 10,000 to 12,500 words and an oral defence, guided by a faculty supervisor.

Starts with Senior Thesis: Proposal, Research & Ethics.

Three very different endings. The Social Action Project puts you inside a community organisation. The Multi-Stakeholder Simulation scales up the Policy Challenge: a national emergency or a collapsing regional economy, with no single actor able to act alone. The Senior Thesis is the most important piece of writing an undergraduate completes, according to the guide, and it begins with a proposal course that includes ethical approval. About 1.5 minutes.
